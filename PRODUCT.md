## Platform

web

## Users

- **Wholesale and retail buyers** who review, select and order the Ralph Lauren × Tour de France capsule collection for their stores.
- **Store teams** who receive, present and sell the capsule and need merchandising guidance and approved assets.
- **Ralph Lauren account teams** who send buyers to the site and need it to represent Ralph Lauren faithfully.

They arrive with a task (review the capsule, download a line sheet, find their account contact), on a work laptop or on a phone in the store.

## Product Purpose

A Ralph Lauren destination for the capsule collection created for the 2027 Tour de France and offered to wholesale partners: the collection, buyer resources (line sheets, lookbook, visual merchandising guide), ordering and contacts.

## Positioning

Ralph Lauren is the host brand and the Tour de France is the guest of honour. The Tour appears in the lockup, in its yellow (as title bars over photography, a pale jaune ground and, at most once per page, a maillot gold band) and in race photography, never in the structure, type or voice.
