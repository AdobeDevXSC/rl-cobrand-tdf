From Showroom to Shop Floor

The capsule was designed to be seen as a whole. This guide shows how to place it on the floor, dress the fixtures and build the window, so it looks the same in every store as it did in the showroom.

The Floor

Fixtures & Folding

Follow the fixture plans for each store format and the folding standards for knitwear and polos, from light to dark.

Step 03

The Window

Build the window from the lookbook imagery and the approved Ralph Lauren × Tour de France signage, with one hero look per window.
